# Portfolio typography

Coolvetica Regular leads the portfolio headings and introduction. Stretch Pro is reserved for the short contact headline. Geist remains the body and UI typeface, with Space Grotesk as the display fallback.

The owner confirmed purchase of the licenses on November 16, 2026 and supplied the font files. Both faces are served as compressed WOFF with font-display: swap and their real regular weight, 400. Font families and all affected selectors are specific to the portfolio; shared font declarations and Studio are unchanged.

Sources: Coolvetica Rg.otf and StretchPro.otf supplied by the owner. License documents and purchase information are not published in this repository.
